# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People managing personal files in a focused web dashboard. They need to upload, browse, search, share, and remove files quickly.

## Product Purpose

BasketStar provides a simple, reliable file-management workspace backed by Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage.

## Brand Personality

Direct, practical, confident.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy gradients, fragile decorative UI, low-contrast labels, and controls that do not behave like familiar product controls.

## Design Principles

- Keep file operations easy to scan and quick to reach.
- Preserve consistent surfaces and control states across light and dark themes.
- Use the existing red/black identity as a restrained accent, not decoration everywhere.
- Keep metadata, ownership, and file actions legible at a glance.
- Treat accessibility and clear feedback as part of the product, not polish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support.
